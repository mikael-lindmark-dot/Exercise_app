# Content Research and Starter Exercise Library

This file captures a starter content direction from the imported project conversation. It is not a substitute for professional exercise programming review.

## Tracks

### Military Calisthenics

Purpose: bodyweight strength, muscular endurance, core stability, and conditioning.

Beginner examples:

- Incline push-up
- Wall push-up
- Assisted squat to chair
- Dead bug
- Glute bridge
- Marching in place
- Forearm plank from knees
- Step-back lunge support hold

Intermediate examples:

- Standard push-up
- Bodyweight squat
- Reverse lunge
- Mountain climber
- Plank shoulder tap
- Sit-up or curl-up
- Bear crawl hold
- Burpee step-back

Advanced examples:

- Hand-release push-up
- Jump squat
- Walking lunge
- Full burpee
- Side plank rotation
- Hollow-body hold
- Tempo push-up
- Conditioning circuit intervals

### Chair Tai Chi

Purpose: seated mobility, breath, coordination, balance confidence, posture, and nervous-system downshifting.

Beginner examples:

- Seated breathing with posture reset
- Seated neck release
- Seated shoulder circles
- Seated wrist circles
- Seated ankle circles
- Seated weight shift
- Seated cloud hands
- Seated open-and-close movement

Intermediate examples:

- Seated brush knee pattern
- Seated parting the wild horse's mane
- Chair-supported standing weight shift
- Chair-supported heel raise
- Chair-supported side step
- Seated spinal rotation
- Slow seated knee lift
- Coordinated breath and arm sweep

Advanced examples:

- Chair-supported single-leg balance
- Standing cloud hands with chair nearby
- Slow controlled sit-to-stand
- Chair-supported tai chi flow sequence
- Standing heel-to-toe weight transfer
- Diagonal arm sweep with trunk rotation
- Slow lateral stepping pattern
- Long-form breath-led sequence

### Everyday Strength & Mobility

Purpose: daily-life movement capacity, joint mobility, gentle strength, and confidence.

Beginner examples:

- Sit-to-stand from chair
- Wall calf raise
- Standing hip circle with chair support
- Doorway chest opener
- Seated hamstring reach
- Wall angel
- Farmer carry simulation
- Supported side bend

Intermediate examples:

- Step-up
- Hip hinge drill
- Resistance-band row
- Standing calf raise
- Bird dog
- Side step with band
- Split squat to support
- Loaded carry

Advanced examples:

- Single-leg Romanian deadlift support
- Goblet squat
- Tempo step-up
- Turkish get-up progression
- Resistance-band complex
- Lateral lunge
- Loaded carry intervals
- Mobility flow sequence

## Exercise data fields

Each exercise should eventually include:

- id
- name
- track
- level
- duration or reps
- equipment
- primary muscle or movement focus
- setup instructions
- movement cues
- breathing cues
- easier alternative
- harder alternative
- contraindication notes
- trainer media reference
- caption text

## Safety guidance

Production content should include review by qualified professionals, especially for:

- Older adults.
- Users with pain or mobility limitations.
- Balance-impaired users.
- High-intensity calisthenics sessions.
- Any medical or rehabilitation-adjacent guidance.

Initial public-health and exercise references to consult during production:

- NHS sitting exercises.
- NHS strength exercises.
- NHS balance exercises.
- NHS older-adult physical activity guidance.
- US Army physical readiness material for calisthenics inspiration.
- VA or clinically reviewed Tai Chi resources for seated/standing Tai Chi guidance.
