# Project Brief: Evolve Exercise App

## Imported source

This brief imports the working context from the referenced ChatGPT conversation “Exercise App Development.”

The user wants to build a personalised exercise app similar in spirit to popular App Store fitness apps, but with a calmer, more inclusive design direction and three distinct training tracks:

1. Military Calisthenics
2. Chair Tai Chi
3. Everyday Strength & Mobility

The selected visual direction is Design 1: Calm, inclusive agenda.

## Product concept

Evolve should feel like an adaptive training companion. It should help users decide what to do today, understand why a session was recommended, adapt it to current readiness, and track meaningful progress over time.

The app should not feel like a generic video library. The centre of the product is a personalised weekly plan that adapts to user goals, fitness level, available equipment, mobility needs, and post-session feedback.

## Target audience

The app should work for a mixed user base:

- Users interested in military-style bodyweight conditioning.
- Older adults or lower-mobility users who benefit from chair-based movement.
- Everyday users who want strength, mobility, confidence, and consistency.
- Users returning from inactivity who need gentle progression.

## Design direction

The chosen design style should be:

- Calm
- Inclusive
- Accessible
- Warm
- Spacious
- Capability-led
- Less “elite gym,” more “trusted daily coach”

Suggested visual traits:

- Forest green primary actions.
- Terracotta secondary accents.
- Soft neutral backgrounds.
- Editorial serif headings.
- Highly readable sans-serif body text.
- Large tap targets.
- Supportive, non-shaming copy.
- Diverse trainer imagery, including older adults, women, men, and non-white trainers.

## Top screens

### 1. Today

The daily entry point. Shows the recommended session, user readiness check, adaptation options, and weekly context.

### 2. Weekly Plan

Shows the planned training week across all three tracks, with recovery and mobility work balanced against strength and conditioning.

### 3. Session Overview

Explains the selected session before it starts: duration, purpose, equipment, exercises, alternatives, safety guidance, and why it was recommended.

### 4. Active Workout

Guided session player with trainer image/video area, timer, movement cue, captions, pause, skip, previous/next, easier alternative, and safety controls.

### 5. Progress

Shows consistency, strength, balance, mobility, completed sessions, milestones, and qualitative wins. Avoids over-reliance on calories, weight, or leaderboards.

## MVP feature set

- Onboarding assessment.
- Six-week adaptive plans.
- Today screen and weekly agenda.
- Guided workout player.
- Exercise alternatives.
- Session adaptation.
- Post-session effort, pain, and enjoyment check-in.
- Progress milestones.
- Exercise and programme content model.
- Starter subscription-ready product structure.

## Adaptation model

The initial recommendation engine can be rules-based. It should consider:

- Preferred track.
- Ability level.
- Available time.
- Equipment.
- Recent training load.
- Soreness or pain feedback.
- Mobility/balance needs.
- Session completion history.
- User enjoyment and perceived effort.

Machine learning can come later once real user behaviour exists.
